1.Who this policy covers

This policy is issued by Sorted Innovations Limited, a company registered in Nigeria, RC 9770241 ("Enough", "we", "us"), and explains how we handle personal data for anyone who creates an Enough account. We process personal data as a data controller in line with Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023 and the regulations of the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

At signup, you confirm you've read this policy, our Terms of Service, and our Data Protection & Data Use Agreement by ticking a box before your account is created — your account cannot be created without it.

3.How we use it

  • To open, verify and operate your account, including with our payment processing partner(s).
  • To generate and let you edit your spending plan.
  • To process deposits and the payments you ask us to send.
  • To send you service messages — deposit and payment confirmations, verification prompts, support replies.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, and to meet our legal, tax, AML/CFT and regulatory obligations.
  • To understand how Enough is used, so we can improve it.

We don't sell your personal data, and we don't use your description, plan, or transaction data to train third-party advertising products.

4.Your description and Enough's AI

When you describe your life to build a plan, that description is sent to a third-party AI provider (currently OpenAI) to generate a suggested set of purposes and percentages, which is then returned to you for review before it's applied to your account. OpenAI processes this text to generate that response; it isn't used by Enough or by OpenAI to identify you beyond what's in the text itself.

Because this provider is based outside Nigeria, this is an international transfer of personal data — see Section 11.

5.Your BVN specifically

We collect your BVN to verify your identity, in line with Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) Know-Your-Customer requirements, and pass it to our payment processing partner(s) and, through them, to the Nigeria Inter-Bank Settlement System (NIBSS), for that verification. We don't use your BVN for any purpose beyond identity verification and the regulatory checks it's collected for, and we don't share it with anyone outside that process. See our Data Protection & Data Use Agreement for the specific consent this relies on.

7.How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as your Enough account is open. After you close your account, we keep transaction records and identity verification data for at least five (5) years, in line with CBN anti-money-laundering recordkeeping requirements, even though your account itself is closed. We delete your plan description and purpose data once it's no longer needed for that retention period.

8.Your rights

Under Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023, you can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct it, if it's wrong.
  • Delete your account and the data tied to it, subject to the retention we describe in Section 7.
  • Restrict or object to certain uses of your data.
  • Give you your plan and transaction data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent you've given us, where our use of your data relies on it — see our Data Protection & Data Use Agreement for what withdrawing consent means for your account.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) if you believe we've mishandled your data.

9.Security

Passwords are stored hashed, not in plain text. Your BVN and payment details are handled by our licensed payment processing partner(s), not stored in plain form on Enough's own systems. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your data and limit internal access to what each part of the team needs to do its job.

10.Children

Enough is for people 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18; if we learn we have, we'll delete it and close the account.

11.International transfers

Our AI plan generation provider (currently OpenAI) may process your description outside Nigeria. Where this happens, we rely on that provider's own safeguards and published data processing terms, consistent with the Data Protection Act's requirements for transferring personal data outside Nigeria.

12.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Enough changes. For material changes, we'll give notice in the app or by message before they take effect.
