Data Protection & Data Use Agreement

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Enough asks for two unusually sensitive things: your Bank Verification Number, and a plain-language description of your financial life that we send to an AI model to build your plan. This agreement spells out exactly what that means, separately from our general Privacy Policy, so your consent to each one is explicit and informed.

1.What this agreement is for

Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023 and Central Bank of Nigeria rules on BVN use both require your clear, specific, informed consent before we use particularly sensitive data in particular ways — not just a general acknowledgment buried in a privacy policy. This agreement is that specific consent, for two things: your BVN, and the description you give us to build your spending plan. It's issued by Sorted Innovations Limited, RC 9770241, the operator of Enough.

2.Your BVN — what we use it for

Explicit consent

By ticking the box at signup, you consent to Enough collecting your Bank Verification Number (BVN) and sending it to our payment processing partner(s) and, through them, to the Nigeria Inter-Bank Settlement System (NIBSS), solely to verify your identity and activate your Enough account, in line with Central Bank of Nigeria Know-Your-Customer requirements.

We don't use your BVN to look up or access any other bank account you hold, and we don't share it with anyone beyond this verification process. Your BVN isn't used for marketing, credit scoring, or any purpose other than the one described here.

3.Your plan description and AI processing

Explicit consent

By ticking the box at signup, you consent to the description you provide when building or editing your Enough plan being sent to a third-party AI provider (currently OpenAI) so it can generate a suggested set of purposes and percentages for your review.

This description can include details about your income, dependants, debts, and spending habits. We send only what you type into the plan-building field — not your BVN, your account number, or your transaction history — to the AI provider. The suggested plan it returns is never applied to your account automatically; you review and approve it first, and can edit or reject it.

Because this AI provider is based outside Nigeria, this also involves an international transfer of your description — see our Privacy Policy, Section 11.

4.Account and transaction data

Separately from the two explicit consents above, operating your account necessarily involves sharing your account activity — deposits, purpose balances, and payments you send — with our payment processing partner(s) and the Nigerian banking network, so that money can actually move in and out of your account. This is necessary to provide Enough to you at all, and isn't something you can opt out of while keeping an active account; our Privacy Policy explains it in full.

5.The lawful basis we rely on

For your BVN and your plan description, we rely on your explicit consent, given at signup and recorded against your account. For operating your account and moving money, we rely on the performance of our contract with you (these terms) and on our legal obligations as a regulated payment service user, including AML/CFT recordkeeping.

6.Withdrawing your consent

You can withdraw your consent to AI processing of your plan description at any time by contacting us — we'll stop sending new descriptions to the AI provider, though your account and any previously generated plan stay in place, and you can still edit your purposes manually.

You can't withdraw consent to BVN verification while keeping an active account, because it's required by law to operate one — withdrawing it means closing your Enough account instead. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect anything we did with your data before you withdrew it.

7.How this relates to our other policies

This agreement works alongside our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy — it doesn't replace them. Where this agreement and the Privacy Policy both describe how we use your BVN or your plan description, this agreement is the more specific and controlling statement of your consent for those two uses.

8.Your acknowledgment

At signup, you confirm:
  • I've read and understood this Data Protection & Data Use Agreement, the Terms of Service, and the Privacy Policy.
  • I consent to Enough collecting and verifying my BVN as described in Section 2.
  • I consent to my plan description being sent to a third-party AI provider as described in Section 3.
  • I understand I can withdraw my AI-processing consent at any time, as described in Section 6.
