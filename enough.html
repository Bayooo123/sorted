The problem

Payday feels rich. Week three feels different.

Week 1

Salary lands. Everything feels possible.

Owambe, outings, the small treats.
Week 2

Bills, data, family requests.

The balance starts to shrink.
Week 3

“Where did it all go?”

Careful spending, quiet worry.
Week 4

Borrow, beg or wait.

Salary advance. Loan app. Friends.

It isn’t always about how much you earn. It’s that nothing tells your money where to go before it goes. Banks show one balance, savings apps lock some away, and the rest is left to chance.

How it works

A plan for every naira,
made in minutes.

No spreadsheets, no budgeting homework. You talk; Enough plans; you approve.

Describe yourself

Your work, when you’re paid, your rent, the people you support, your goals. In plain words, or by voice.

Choose your level

Flexible, Balanced or Strict. Set your limits now, while you’re calm.

Approve your plan

Pay by purpose

Pay any bank, OPay or Moniepoint account from the right purpose. Your plan keeps you on track.

You set the rules

Decide how strict, while you’re calm.

Flexible

Gentle nudges for people who mostly need a plan.

  • Move money between purposes anytime
  • Pay any account
  • Reminders when you drift
Most chosen

Balanced

Short pauses before you break your own plan.

  • Over a cap? Top up from free money
  • New payees flagged
  • 2-hour wait to unlock a purpose

Strict

For when you really mean it this month.

  • Saved payees only
  • Payments over a cap declined
  • 24-hour wait to unlock anything

Tighten anytime. Loosening takes effect after 24 hours, so your calm self stays in charge.

Questions

Good to know.

Where is my money kept?

In an account in your name with our licensed banking partner, [Partner bank]. Enough organises it into purposes; it never sits in Enough’s own account.

Can I withdraw my money anytime?

Yes. Free money is always available. Purposes you’ve locked follow the level you chose, so on Strict, unlocking takes 24 hours. That pause is the point.

Do I need to change my bank?

No. Keep your bank. Send your salary, or part of it, to your Enough account and we plan it from there.

Can I pay people who don’t use Enough?

Yes. Pay any Nigerian bank, OPay or Moniepoint account from the right purpose.

How much does it cost?

Enough is free to start for individuals. Business plans and extra features will be paid. We’ll share pricing before launch.
