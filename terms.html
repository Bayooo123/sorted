3.How Sorted works

A client posts a gig with the work needed, the location and the budget. A professional claims it, or a client hires one directly from the directory. Once the professional submits the work and the client approves it, payment releases automatically through our payment partner (currently Paystack and/or Bachs) — the professional is paid directly and Sorted's fee comes out of the same transaction, so your money is never held by Sorted in between. We don't see or store your card details. Where a gig runs on a manual, pilot-only payment process instead, we'll say so clearly before you pay.

Sorted provides the marketplace, payment handling and dispute support. We're not a party to the underlying agreement between a client and a professional, and we don't perform the work ourselves.

4.Delivery and pickup

For categories where Sorted arranges pickup and delivery (for example, laundry and dry cleaning), we share the pickup and drop-off address and a contact phone number with our delivery partner so they can dispatch a courier. Delivery timing depends on the courier and isn't guaranteed to the minute.

5.Fees

Sorted charges a platform fee on completed gigs, shown before you pay — currently up to 10% of the gig's bounty, waived for categories where Sorted already arranges paid delivery. The fee that applies is the one shown when you approve payment.

6.Cancellations, disputes and refunds

If something's wrong with the work, raise a dispute before approving it — approving releases payment and can't be reversed through the app. A dispute pauses the gig until it's reviewed. If it's resolved for the client, nothing is charged. If it's resolved for the professional, we'll ask the client to pay, though Nigerian payment rails mean we can't force that charge — resolution in that case relies on each side's record on Sorted rather than a guaranteed payout. Outside a dispute, a gig can be cancelled before work starts by agreement between both sides.
