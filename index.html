How it works

Three steps. Money moves only on the last one.

Post a gig

Describe the job and the criteria that define “done.” Once published, those criteria lock — no moving the goalposts.

Fund it in escrow

The bounty is held before anyone starts work — not with Sorted, not with the payer. Safely in the middle, always.

Sign off, get paid

The payer confirms each criterion is met. Escrow releases automatically — solver paid, stake returned.

Transparent by design

Every naira, accounted for before work even starts.

No hidden cuts. Sorted's fee is fixed and shown up front — the same math the payer sees when funding, and the solver sees when claiming. Nothing changes after criteria are locked.
