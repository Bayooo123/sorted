Describe the job and set the criteria that define "done." Once published, those criteria are locked.

2

The bounty is held in escrow before anyone starts work — not with Sorted, not with the payer, safely in the middle.

The payer confirms each criterion is met. Escrow releases automatically — solver gets paid, stake returned.
