2.What Enough is — and isn't

Enough gives you a real bank account number, lets you describe your income and expenses in plain language, and uses that description to suggest a spending plan — named "purposes" (like rent, data, family, savings) each holding a share of what you earn. Deposits into your Enough account split across those purposes automatically, and you pay people directly out of a purpose.

Enough is not a deposit money bank and doesn't hold a banking license. Your account number and the actual movement of money — receiving deposits, holding your balance, and sending payments — is provided by one or more licensed payment processing partners (currently Paga, with Bachs and Nomba available as secondary rails). We'll tell you which partner your account runs on if you ask. Your money is held and safeguarded under that partner's own licensing arrangements, not Sorted Innovations Limited's.

Your "purposes" are a feature of Enough's own bookkeeping, layered on top of one underlying account — they are not separate bank accounts, and a purpose isn't separately deposit-insured from the others.

4.How Enough works — your plan and purposes

When you describe your life — what you earn, who depends on it, what it needs to cover — Enough uses that description to draft a plan: a set of named purposes, each with a percentage of your income. You review and approve this plan before it takes effect, and you can edit it at any time.

Once your plan is active, a deposit into your Enough account is split across your purposes according to the percentages you approved. You can move money between purposes, and you can set part of a purpose's balance aside on a schedule ("tranches") so it isn't available for everyday spending until the date you choose.

5.AI-generated plans aren't financial advice

The plan Enough suggests is generated by an AI model from the description you provide. It's a starting point, not professional financial, tax, or legal advice, and we don't guarantee it's the best or most tax-efficient way to divide your money. You're responsible for reviewing a suggested plan before approving it, and for every payment you make from a purpose afterward.

See our Privacy Policy for how your description is processed to generate a plan, including by a third-party AI provider.

6.Payments to other people

You can pay another Nigerian bank account directly from a purpose's available balance (its balance minus anything you've locked into a tranche). You're responsible for entering the right account details — Enough shows you the recipient's name returned by the banking network before you confirm, but a payment sent to the wrong account because of details you entered can't always be reversed once it leaves the Enough account.

A payment may show as "processing" briefly while our payment processing partner confirms it went through, and we'll update the payment's status and notify you once it has.
